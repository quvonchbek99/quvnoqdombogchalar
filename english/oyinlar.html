Inglizcha gapda so'z tartibi qat'iy

O'zbekchada fe'l gap oxirida keladi: «Men futbol o'ynayman». Inglizchada esa fe'l egadan keyin darhol turadi: «I play football».

Ega→Fe'l→To'ldiruvchi→Joy · Vaqt
DarakI play football in the park on Sundays.Men yakshanba kunlari bog'da futbol o'ynayman.
SavolDo you like tea?Savolda yordamchi fe'l (do, does, did, is, are, can, will) egadan oldin keladi.
InkorShe doesn't like milk.Inkor: don't / doesn't / didn't / isn't / can't + fe'l.

Zamonni signal so'z aytib beradi

Fe'l shakli harakat qachon bo'lishini ko'rsatadi. Gapdagi signal so'zni toping — u harakat vaqt chizig'ining qayerida ekanini va qaysi zamon kerakligini aytadi.

⏪ Past SimpleShe visited her grandma yesterday.yesterday, last week, ago → V2 yoki -ed
▶ Present ContinuousLook! The baby is sleeping.now, Look!, at the moment → am/is/are + V-ing
🔁 Present SimpleHe plays chess every Sunday.every day, usually, always → V (he/she/it: V-s)
⏩ Future SimpleI think it will rain tomorrow.tomorrow, I think, in the future → will + V

Fe'lning 3 ta shakli: V1 → V2 → V3

V1 — oddiy shakl (go), V2 — o'tgan zamon (went), V3 — have/has bilan va majhul nisbatda (gone, written). To'g'ri fe'llar -ed oladi: play → played → played. Noto'g'ri fe'llarni yodlash kerak, lekin ular 4 guruhga bo'linadi: AAA cut–cut–cut, ABB buy–bought–bought, ABC go–went–gone, ABA come–came–come.

V2I went to school yesterday.Kecha maktabga bordim. (Past Simple)
V3She has written a letter.U xat yozib bo'ldi. (have/has + V3)
V3The book was written in 2020.Kitob 2020-yilda yozilgan. (majhul nisbat)

So'zni ko'r, eshit, ayt va yoz

So'z xotirada 4 xil yo'l bilan mustahkamlanadi: rasm va tarjima → talaffuz → aytish → harflab yozish. Inglizchada yozilishi va o'qilishi ko'pincha farq qiladi — shuning uchun so'zni eshitib yozish (spelling bee) juda foydali.

knife /naɪf/ — pichoqk o'qilmaydi: know, knee, knock ham shunday.
hour /ˈaʊə/ — soath o'qilmaydi, shuning uchun an hour.
write /raɪt/ → wrotew o'qilmaydi; V2 da unli o'zgaradi.
