📅 Kunning misollari —

Milliy sertifikat test formatida har kuni 10 ta yangi misol: 7 ta algebra (51–70) va 3 ta geometriya (71–88), osondan qiyinga. Yarim tunda o'zi yangilanadi va hamma o'quvchida bir xil — sinfda birga yechib, natijani solishtirish mumkin. Misollar saytning o'z generatorlarida tuziladi (kitoblardan ko'chirilmagan).

    📝 Sinov imtihoni

    Darslikning 15 bobidan tanlangan 20 mavzu (51–70) bo'yicha aralash savollar. Rasmiy format va sanalar: uzbmb.uz.

    📚 Mavzular (51–70) — har birida alohida test va o'yinlar

    🗓 4 haftalik tayyorlov rejasi

    1. 1-hafta — Sonlar va algebra asoslari: 61, 56, 57, 51, 52, 62 · har kuni 1 mavzu: xossalar → «Oson» test 10 ta → «O'rta» 20 ta.
    2. 2-hafta — Tenglama va tengsizliklar: 53, 63, 64, 65, 66, 67 · kuniga 2 ta «O'rta» test, xatolarni daftarga yozish.
    3. 3-hafta — Funksiya, trigonometriya, progressiya: 54, 55, 58, 68, 69 · «Murakkab» testlar, Desmos bilan grafiklar.
    4. 4-hafta — Hosila, integral va umumiy sinov: 59, 60, 70 · har kuni 1 ta 40 savollik sinov imtihoni, «Nimani mustahkamlash kerak» bo'yicha qaytish.

    💡 SAT strategy

    1. Start with «Short — 10 questions» to get a feel for the timing: about 1.5 minutes per question.
    2. Do a «Medium» test of 20 questions in each domain; below 80% — go back to the topic's properties.
    3. Take a full 44-question practice test twice a week and read the solution steps in the review.
    4. Desmos is the calculator allowed on the SAT: go through the Desmos lessons (7 lessons with exercises); every SAT test has a 🧮 Desmos button.
