English

The game collects no personal data and sends no data to anyone over the internet. It has no accounts, advertising or tracking.

Settings, results and flight recordings stay on your computer. Pictures taken in the game are saved to "Pictures\DronUz M50". The game uses no camera or microphone.

If you turn on the option to use a phone as the controller, the game receives control inputs from devices on your home network. This data is not stored and does not leave your home network. The connection is not encrypted, so turn it on only on a network you trust.

To delete the data, uninstall the game in Windows; delete pictures yourself. Data collected by the Microsoft Store itself is covered by Microsoft's privacy statement.
